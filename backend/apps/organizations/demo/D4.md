## Catalog and chronology choices

V1 D4 supports the explicit synthetic-plan combination species code BOV and breed code HOL and production type MILK with unit L. These are seeder plan restrictions, not new domain rules. They must exist and be active; tests explicitly supply the BOV/HOL fixtures. Other species/type combinations are refused until a separate coherent plan is defined. Weighing and reproduction method codes must be supplied explicitly; tests use SCALE and NAT fixtures. TRANSFER, SALE, SOLD, ACTIVE, F and M must be active. No catalog is created or repaired by D4.

Dates derive from tenant business_today. Historical weights, vaccinations and movements use the earlier of tenant today, Django localdate and Mexico today to satisfy the existing heterogeneous service contracts. No clocks or validators are patched by the component. The illustrative bovine timeline places reproduction 280 days and diagnosis 200 days before the expected birth, including active forecasts; these are synthetic plan choices, not new service rules. Parents predate events. Automatic and non-live births use tenant today; existing live births use each D3 calf's recorded birth_date and parents. Every pregnancy has a distinct eligible mother/event, and at most one birth.

Each weighed adult has two dates (historical and recent) with different positive values. San José receives no weights, so average_weight is null; all organizations have animals without weights. Vaccination labels are explicitly DEMO synthetic, never clinical protocols. Next applications cycle through overdue, today, future and null.

Active pregnancy forecasts include today, +30 (where there are forecasts), intermediate dates and +31 outside the window. San José has no within-window forecasts. Production uses lactating automatic-birth mothers at tenant civil midnight today (aware timestamp, never future). Quantities are synthetic test values and unit comes from ProductionService's catalog resolution. Juveniles are selected for one transfer and final sales; reproductive mothers/males/calves are never sold or moved. Transfer destination has spare capacity, and final sales remove location via the movement service.

## Receipts and protection

D4 verifies the intact signed D2/D3 state before fresh creation. Since D4 legitimately changes demo animal location/status, repeats verify D3's signed original receipt plus D4's signed before/after transition chain and complete current inventory, rather than invoking D3's unchanged post-D3-only verifier against a post-D4 database. D2 accounts and D3 farms/lots remain unchanged. Only planned movement animals may change location/status; all other D3 animal fields remain immutable. Every new record, automatic calf, pregnancy outcome and transition is included in D4 inventory. Data outside the authorized demo UUIDs and catalogs are snapshotted and protected.

Publication occurs after SQL commit. A publication failure cannot safely roll back a committed transaction; it retains the encrypted pending receipt for verified recovery. Errors before commit request global rollback. Missing, partial, altered or unsigned datasets are rejected without repair or reseeding. This adjusts the requested “rollback on publication failure” to the real SQL/filesystem boundary; services are unchanged.

The implementation and tests remain confined to disposable test databases. Windows publication, real ACL/key custody and power-loss durability retain D2's documented limitations. D5 must certify scenarios and delivery policy before any future real execution.
